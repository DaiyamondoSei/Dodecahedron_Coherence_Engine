What is this?

Quannex maps your organization onto a dodecahedron - a 12-faced Platonic solid that ancient Greeks associated with the cosmos. Each face represents a domain of your organization (finance, team, operations...). The edges show how domains connect. The vertices reveal where three domains converge to create leverage points. Together, they reveal your organization's coherence - or lack thereof.
