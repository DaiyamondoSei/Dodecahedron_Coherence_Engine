## About This Project: Quannex

This is the **Quannex POC** - a Sacred Geometry Organizational Coherence Engine. It measures organizational health through:

- **12 Faces** of a dodecahedron (12 organizational domains)
- **5 Elements** per face (pentagram geometry)
- **6 Breath Axes** (balanced flow between domains)
- **7 Octaves** of development (Survival -> Radiance)
- **Golden Ratio (phi)** throughout

This is Deimantas's **Bachelor's thesis** (February 2025 defense). The project uses Three.js for 3D visualization, Gemini AI for insights, and sacred geometry mathematics.
