## What Quannex Actually Is

Quannex is a **paradigm shift** in understanding organizations.

Instead of treating companies as machines to be optimized, Quannex treats them as **living, breathing organisms** that:

### 1. Have 12 Faces (Organizational Dimensions)
Like a dodecahedron - one of the five Platonic solids representing wholeness:
- Financial Capital, Intellectual Capital, Human Capital, Structural Capital
- Market Resonance, Community & Partners, Brand & Reputation, Core Operations
- Regenerative Flow, Foundational Values, Funding Pipeline, Risk & Resilience

### 2. Breathe Through 6 Axes
Pairs of opposite faces that balance reception (inhale) and projection (exhale):
- **Resource Flow** - Money in ↔ money out ("The breath of money")
- **Substance & Story** - Building IP ↔ communicating value ("The breath of knowledge")
- **Being & Doing** - Developing people ↔ producing work ("The breath of work")
- **Form & Integrity** - Renewing foundations ↔ building systems ("The breath of structure")
- **Perception & Truth** - Grounding in values ↔ market validation ("The breath of integrity")
- **Network & Fortress** - Building resilience ↔ collaboration ("The breath of boundaries")

Over-exhaling = burnout, depletion
Over-inhaling = stagnation, unexpressed potential
Health = balanced rhythm

### 3. Develop Through 7 Octaves
Like a human evolving from survival to self-actualization:
- **O1: Survival** (Fighting to exist)
- **O2: Structure** (Building stability)
- **O3: Relationships** (Connection)
- **O4: Creativity** (Possibility)
- **O5: Expression** (Clarity)
- **O6: Vision** (Direction)
- **O7: Radiance** (Service)

Thresholds use the golden ratio (φ):
- O2 starts at φ⁻² = 0.382
- O4 starts at φ⁻¹ = 0.618

### 4. Coherence Over Optimization
The question isn't "how do we maximize outputs?"
The question is "how harmoniously are all 12 dimensions working together?"
