Week 50 Update: We shipped "organizational shadows"

Every organization has blind spots. Dimensions that look healthy on the surface but carry hidden imbalances underneath.

We call these shadows.

This week we built the Shadow Detection System for Quannex - and I want to share what it actually does:

**What it detects:**
- Faces (organizational dimensions) that appear strong but show early warning signs
- Imbalances in the breath axes before they become critical
- Patterns that indicate potential burnout or stagnation

**How it presents this:**
- Expandable detail panels - click to understand the "why" behind each shadow
- AI-assisted interpretation - context-aware explanations, not just numbers
- User-respectful approach - insights, not judgments

The philosophy behind this feature:

Shadows aren't failures. They're invitations to see more clearly.

An organization operating at O3 (Relationships stage) with shadows in its Financial dimension isn't "bad at money" - it might be appropriately focused on connection while needing a gentle reminder to tend to cash flow.

Building this reminded me why we're doing this work: Not to judge organizations, but to help them see themselves with more clarity and compassion.

7 phases completed. CSS architecture documented. AI integration respectful.

Onward.
