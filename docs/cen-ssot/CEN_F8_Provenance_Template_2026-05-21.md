### The BSC counterfactual (what a single-scorer BSC would report at F8)

Under a typical BSC implementation, F8 would be reported as ONE of the following (depending on convention):

**(a) Single-respondent BSC** (most common at small NGOs/early-stage orgs): One co-founder (typically the operational lead — Dominique in CEN's case) completes the BSC. **In all three BSC conventions: the 6-point gap is invisible as a finding.** Either one perspective dominates (a), or the perspectives are arithmetically averaged (b), or they're negotiated to consensus (c).
